# YY Outfits — Aligned v2

108 individual transparent PNGs: 54 in `tier-40/` and 54 in `tier-60/`. Each selected outfit has normal, happy and sad states in light-peach, warm-golden and dark-brown skin tones. The two ZIPs contain the same individual PNGs plus exact generation prompts, provenance and registration records. The overview PNGs show every combination.

All final images use the existing tier-20 600 × 800 canvas. The reviewed scalp-to-chin head rectangle is x96, y132, width395, height335 in every image, with a shared planted-foot baseline at y760. Both bodies and future hair overlays can therefore use the same canvas origin. Goggles and glasses remain; the head measurements exclude eyewear protrusions. Body positions below the chin are normalized continuously to maintain the same head-to-body height ratio without a gap at the neck. Nearest-neighbor sampling keeps the pixel-art edges crisp.

23 Construction Worker A and 42 Police Officer A happy/dark-brown skin shading was corrected against their approved normal/sad dark-brown references. The remaining art keeps its original clothing and expression. Hair and hats remain removed; number23 has no beard. The sad state follows the third base-sheet figure with a worried hand-near-mouth pose and no tears.

Tier40 selections: 11 Art Student C; 27 Farmer A; 49 Soccer Player; 45 School Captain; 38 Mother B; 31 Girl A.

Tier60 selections: 23 Construction Worker A; 35 Lifeguard A; 40 Nurse A; 42 Police Officer A; 47 Scientist A; 15 B Teacher B.

This is an asset review package. It does not change the live XY Learning Lab application, any handoff document, database, outfit interface or animations. Tier80 assets and its future animation changes are outside this package. Original unaligned v1 artwork stays preserved in the local workspace.
