# Playground source audit

Read-only source: `Playground.pptx` and `Playground.pdf`, 14 slides, 16:9. Every page was rendered as `slide-01.png` through `slide-14.png` and visually inspected. Most original wording is baked into the JPEG backgrounds, so the text below is a manual visual transcription, not automatic PDF text. Original editable PPTX objects mostly consist of empty answer boxes, copyright, link buttons and equipment pictures on slide 12. All fixed equipment expressions below were derived from the slide dimensions and then independently confirmed in the Google Form editor. The accidental basketball answer string `Correct Answer` is excluded. Final equipment-total and remaining-grass answers have no static key in the Form and are calculated from the chosen blocks.

The original slides have a small `©TruePBL. All rights reserved.` footer. User authorized one small footer beneath the web app instead of repeating on every internal page.

## Meaningful validation requirements suggested by the task

* Grade only long result outputs for interview3–8 and custom item computation (user requirement); scratchwork remains editable and ungraded.
* Normalize whitespace, lettercase, common multiplication symbols and optional units. Accept mathematically equivalent term order, especially `2x+28` and `28+2x`, per explicit user example.
* Do not restrict to one string order copied from Form; reproduce exact intended math and student-friendly alternatives confirmed by Form.
* Carry selection, custom name/image, scratchwork, answers, chosen3, canvas positions and learner name across Previous/Next and refresh.
* Distinguish chosen equipment area sum from playground total and remaining grass area; final checks must recalculate based on current choices.
* The original tiny boxes are scratch and should not block navigation. The interview can offer checks and feedback without deleting work.
